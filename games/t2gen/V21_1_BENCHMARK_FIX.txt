T2GEN V21.1: Fixed command-line benchmark argument parsing.

Commands:
  t2gen /mnt/t2.gen /dev/fb0 40     (40 fixed-snapshot repetitions)
  t2gen /mnt/t2.gen /dev/fb0 0 40   (explicit view=0, 40 repetitions)
  t2gen /mnt/t2.gen /dev/fb0 0      (normal view 0)

Four-argument values 0..5 select view; 6..1000 select benchmark repeats.
Five-argument form allows 2..1000 repeats.

The fixed-snapshot benchmark still runs after the 300-million-cycle emulation.
